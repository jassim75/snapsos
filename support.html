Quick help

  • Join a group: open the invite link, or My groups > Join with a code.
  • Send SOS: press the big SOS button. Your group gets a loud alarm and your live location until you tap "I'm safe".
  • Not getting alerts? Allow notifications and location for SnapSOS (Settings > Phone permissions in the app).
  • Premium: Settings > Premium. Restore purchases is on the same screen. Cancel in your Apple ID subscriptions.
